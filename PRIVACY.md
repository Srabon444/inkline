# Inkline Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Inkline ("the extension") is a Chrome extension that changes the look of text you type by swapping letters for Unicode characters (for example, 𝐁 for B). It is made by MD Ashraful Islam.

## Data the extension handles

| Data | Where it is handled | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The Gmail subject text you choose to style | In your browser, on your device, only when you click a style | To replace it with the styled version |
| The text you select in a text box on another site | In your browser, on your device, only after you pick a style from the right-click menu | To replace it with the styled version |
| The text you type in the toolbar popup | In the popup, on your device | To show the styled previews and copy the one you click |

Inkline does not save any of this text. It is not written to storage, not logged and not kept after the change is made.

## What Inkline does not do

- It has no server. The developer never receives your text or any other data.
- It makes no network requests, and does not load or run remote code.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry.
- It never reads your clipboard. The popup only writes the style you click to it.
- It never changes password fields.
- No data is sold or shared with third parties, and none is used for creditworthiness, lending or any purpose other than styling your text.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Access to `mail.google.com` | Shows the style button in the subject field of the Gmail compose window |
| `contextMenus` | Adds "Inkline style" to the right-click menu of text boxes |
| `activeTab` and `scripting` | Let Inkline restyle the text box you right-clicked, on that tab only, after you choose a style |

## Your control

Inkline keeps no data, so there is nothing to delete. Removing the extension removes it completely.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at https://srabon444.github.io/inkline/ with a new date.
